Introduction

International Conference on Big Data Tools and Techniques

The International Conference on Big Data Tools and Techniques (ICBDTT-2026) is an academic and research conference organized by Sapthagiri NPS University (SNPSU), Bengaluru.

The conference is focused on recent developments, tools, frameworks, and methodologies across Big Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cloud and Distributed Technologies, Data Science, IoT & Edge Computing, and Cybersecurity & Privacy.

Purpose of the Conference

The primary purpose of the conference is to bring together researchers, academicians, students, data scientists, software engineers, and industry professionals from around the world to present and deliberate on original research contributions, state-of-the-art architectures, and innovative data-driven solutions.

Direction

Vision, Objectives & Expected Outcomes

Vision

To create an academic and research forum that promotes excellence in big data analytics, artificial intelligence, and data science, fostering innovations that address complex challenges in science, industry, and society.

Objectives

  • Provide a platform for presentation of peer-reviewed research papers.
  • Discuss practical and theoretical advances in big data architectures and AI.
  • Foster academic-industry knowledge exchange and collaborative research.
  • Highlight emerging trends in cloud analytics, distributed systems, and security.

Expected Outcomes

Publication of high-quality peer-reviewed research, identification of future research directions in big data tools and techniques, and strengthened networking between academic institutions and industry leaders.

Host University

Sapthagiri NPS University (SNPSU)

Sapthagiri NPS University (SNPSU) is an institution of higher education and research located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The university provides quality education across engineering, computing, and emerging disciplines.
