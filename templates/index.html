Conference Overview

Welcome to ICBDTT-2026

The International Conference on Big Data Tools and Techniques (ICBDTT-2026) is organized and hosted by Sapthagiri NPS University (SNPSU), Bengaluru, India. The conference serves as a premier international academic forum for scholars, faculty members, researchers, and technical specialists to present and deliberate on foundational and applied innovations in large-scale data engineering.

With the exponential expansion of digital datasets across healthcare, scientific computing, enterprise platforms, and IoT networks, developing reliable, scalable, and secure data tools is paramount. ICBDTT-2026 brings together original contributions spanning Big Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Distributed Cloud Platforms, Data Science, IoT Edge Computing, and Data Privacy & Cybersecurity.
